Methodology & caveats

2026 values come from Cars.com nationwide asking medians, cross-checked against recent Bring a Trailer sold prints; the adopted figure leans to sold levels (asking × a per-car factor). 2020 baselines and mid-years are reconstructed from documented market lows and the 2021-22 run-up / 2023 wobble / 2024-26 surge — treat the 6-yr % as ±5-8 pts. Projections use momentum-aware CAGRs by each car's place on the modern-classic curve, drivetrain desirability, scarcity, and momentum; bull/bear bands convey uncertainty and are nominal. Maintenance = routine servicing + amortized wear (997 ≈$3k, 458 ≈$5k, F12 ≈$6k, 812s ≈$7k/yr); excludes insurance, storage, fuel, and major repairs. With the toggle on this carrying cost is subtracted from the end value everywhere — the table's 5-yr and 10-yr columns, the returns bars, the YoY bars and the projection table — so the figures show what each car cost to hold, not just what it sold for. Reference series (S&P 500, AAPL) carry no maintenance. Scope vs the asset tracker. The R8 shown here is gated manual, coupe, gen1 V10 only (~$141k all-mileage, ~$118k at 30k+ miles). The asset tracker carries a deliberately broader R8 V10 Gen 1 covering all transmissions and both bodies, which prices lower (~$111k) because R-tronic and S-tronic cars drag the median down. Same generation, different car - not a disagreement. Sold vs asking. The five original cars here are Cars.com asking medians; the five added 2026-08 are Bring a Trailer sold medians, which run lower and are the better evidence. S&P 500 / AAPL are June levels (split-adjusted), not projected. Not investment advice.
